The Gratitude app (**Gratitude: Self-Care Journal**, by Hapjoy Technologies) is a self-care app for iPhone and Android. Besides a gratitude journal, it has affirmations, vision boards and daily motivation cards. Tackbok is a gratitude journal and nothing else.

Both apps keep your entries on your device and don't need an account. The main differences are scope, what's free, and how each app is built.

## Where the Gratitude app is the better choice

- **More self-care tools.** Affirmations with audio, vision boards with music, Daily Zen cards and journaling challenges. Tackbok doesn't try to do these.
- **iCloud.** On iPhone, the Gratitude app can back up to and sync with iCloud. Tackbok's automatic backup uses Google Drive only.
- **A printable journal.** Gratitude Pro can turn your entries into a PDF. Tackbok has no PDF export.
- **A large prompt library.** The Gratitude app offers hundreds of journal prompts.

## Where Tackbok is different

- **Everything is free.** Up to 10 photos per entry, voice memos and entries for any past date cost nothing. Support purchases are optional and unlock no features.
- **Open source.** Anyone can read Tackbok's code on [GitHub](https://github.com/mehradotdev/tackbok).
- **Analytics only if you opt in.** Tackbok collects no usage statistics unless you turn them on, and never collects journal content.
- **Moods and tags.** Record a mood and add tags to any entry, then filter by tag in search.
- **Focused on journaling.** If you only want a gratitude journal, there's less to get in the way.

## A note on backups

The Gratitude app's plans page lists "Backup and sync" in the free plan, while its help centre describes backup as a Pro feature. We've followed the plans page here. Check in the app if it matters to you.

## Switching from the Gratitude app

Tackbok can import the Gratitude app's ZIP export, including photos and voice recordings. Exporting doesn't change your Gratitude journal, so you can try Tackbok with your real entries first. The [Gratitude app import guide](/import/gratitude-app) has the steps and lists what comes across.
