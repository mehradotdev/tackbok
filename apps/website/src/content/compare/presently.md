Presently is a free, ad-free and open-source gratitude journal for Android, made by Alison Wyllie and contributors. It inspired Tackbok, and we think it's a lovely app. If it does what you need, keep using it.

The two biggest differences: Tackbok runs on iPhone as well as Android, and it lets you add photos, voice memos, moods and tags to your entries.

## Presently on iPhone

Presently doesn't have an iPhone app. Its developer said so in 2021 in the project's [GitHub discussion about building an iOS version](https://github.com/alisonthemonster/Presently/discussions/165), and on November 18, 2026 we found no Presently app from its developer on the App Store. Presently is on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=journal.gratitude.com.gratitudejournal).

If you're moving from Android to an iPhone, you can export your Presently entries as a CSV file and import them into Tackbok on the iPhone. The [Presently import guide](/import/presently) has the steps.

## Where Presently is the better choice

- **Simplicity.** Presently is a focused, text-only journal. If you only want to write a few lines each day, there is less to learn.
- **Dropbox backup.** Presently backs up automatically to Dropbox. Tackbok's automatic backup uses Google Drive only.
- **A spreadsheet-friendly export.** Presently's CSV export opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Tackbok's export is a ZIP file meant for restoring into Tackbok.
- **Quotes.** Presently shows quotes as well as prompts for motivation.
- **A long track record.** Presently has been available on Android for years and is still being updated.

## Where Tackbok is different

- **iPhone and Android.** The same journal works on both, and Google Drive sync can keep your devices in step.
- **More than text.** Add photos, voice memos, a mood and tags to any entry, and filter by tag in search.
- **Analytics off by default.** Tackbok only collects anonymous usage statistics if you opt in.
- **Bring your Presently history.** Tackbok imports Presently's CSV export directly, with dates kept.

## Switching from Presently

You don't have to choose straight away. Exporting from Presently doesn't change your Presently journal, so you can try Tackbok with your real entries and keep both apps for a while. Follow the [Presently import guide](/import/presently) to move your entries.
